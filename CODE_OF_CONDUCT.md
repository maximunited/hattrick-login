# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Accepting constructive criticism gracefully
- Focusing on what is best for the community

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, trolling, or insulting comments
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior through a [private security advisory](https://github.com/maximunited/hattrick-login/security/advisories/new) or another private channel to the maintainer.

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions that violate this code of conduct.
